## Primary shipped gameplay contract

Walking players leave server-owned footprint traces: `events/TracesForgeEvents.kt` forwards player ticks and dimension/login/logout events to `server/TraceServerRuntime.kt`, while `logic/CaptureService.kt`, `storage/TraceStorageManager.kt` and `storage/TraceSerializer.kt` implement capture and durable per-world shards. `network/TracesNetwork.kt` serves bounded nearby tile snapshots and annotations; `client/TracesClientHandlers.kt` polls and toggles a reveal overlay, and `client/TracesClientRenderer.kt` renders it. Players can leave/edit shared annotations and follow connected guidance routes (`logic/AnnotationService.kt`, `logic/GuidanceService.kt`); death traces and echoes have server validation, saved data and client rendering (`logic/DeathEchoValidation.kt`, `storage/DeathTraceSavedData.kt`, `client/death/`). Rain and water erode traces (`logic/ErosionService.kt`). These are source-backed behaviors, **not** proof of their appearance or stability in build-69 or in the full pack. `src/echoPrototype` and `verifyEchoPrototype` are explicitly non-shipping prototype work; do not count prototype capabilities as released gameplay.

## Unrun acceptance gaps / ownership of evidence

1. **Local source:** run `verifyFast` and `verifyFull` only after the all-authoring review and human approval. Existing source tests are substantive; no narrowly bounded, non-duplicative regression test was identified in this pass, so none was invented. Do not call test authoring or this inventory completion of the suite.
2. **Native integration blocker:** the 12 GameTests do not establish actual saved-world restart with player login/logout and client reconnect, concurrent visibility and revision consistency for distinct players, or save durability after crash/partial write in a live world. Require a disposable-world cold restart/rejoin and genuine two-client observation, including annotation ownership, footprint visibility, death echo and erosion, with backups and state comparison before reusing a shared world. Do not run native tiers in this authoring phase.
3. **Full pack and player-facing blocker:** verify exact build-69 artifact/provider identity, installation and compat in the full Lineage pack; exercise creation/acquisition (including the missing survival probe route), annotation GUI/key binding, third-party controls, visible occlusion/reveal, cross-dimension travel, death/respawn and server reconnect with actual clients. Source-side success cannot establish render legibility, GUI operability or multiplayer packet delivery.
4. **Scale/performance blocker:** one sampling world per candidate (default seed `424242`), with fake-player loads at **8/16/32** and distances **10** as the campaign specifies, recording TPS/MSPT, memory, packet volume, save queue and trace shard behavior. Those loads are a server-side stress layer, *not* 32 real logins; add true concurrent client joining/polling/edits and logout/rejoin observations separately. No existing GameTest establishes concurrency or a 32-player performance budget. Preserve the world, report any corruption risk first, and do not reset the friends' world to clear a failure.

Qualification remains blocked until the independent pack, native, real-client, persistence and performance evidence is gathered with approval; this file is a test-plan/source contract, not a pass certificate.
